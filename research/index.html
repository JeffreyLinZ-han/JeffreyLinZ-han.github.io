01 · Independent project · Summer 2026

W-D1 Custom 6-DOF Robotic Arm

Open digital twin ↗ Full project page ↗
Hardware motion demo

W-D1 is a from-scratch desktop manipulator that I used to connect mechanical design, actuation, firmware, joint sensing, host-side control, and a MuJoCo digital twin in one system.

STM32PythonMuJoCoSolidWorksURDF/MJCFAbsolute encoders

Mechanical & embedded work

Designed custom joints, timing-belt reductions, bearing and shaft supports, actuator mounts, and printed structural parts. Implemented low-level stepper control on STM32 and a serial host interface for joint commands and debugging.

Simulation & motion

Built a MuJoCo model with the same joint structure as the physical arm and a Python trajectory pipeline. The simulation can execute handwriting paths and serves as the reference for kinematics and motion testing.

Main difficulties

Physical behavior exposed issues that were not obvious in CAD: backlash, belt tension, shaft support, cable routing, joint zeroing, and encoder wrap-around near the raw position boundary. The current work is focused on making commanded and measured joint states agree reliably.

Current state

The hardware can run repeatable demo motions, while the simulation is further ahead in path execution. The next control work is closed-loop joint execution and a cleaner sim-to-hardware interface before adding vision-guided manipulation.

02 · BioMedical Mechatronics Lab · Georgia Tech

Berry Harvesting Robot Simulation

Open model ↗ Full project page ↗

Project scope

The starting point was an existing, mechanically complex harvesting platform. My task was to turn exported CAD and URDF assets into a usable simulation model rather than redesign the physical robot.

Technical work

Processed more than 100 STL components, rebuilt the articulated hierarchy, and corrected mesh orientation, coordinate frames, origins, and joint relationships during the CAD/URDF-to-MuJoCo conversion.

Main difficulty

The challenge was consistency across many inherited parts. A model can look visually correct while the joint frames are wrong, so debugging required separating mesh transforms from kinematic transforms and checking the hierarchy one joint at a time.

Research use

The simulation is being prepared as infrastructure for inverse kinematics, motion planning, fruit perception, visual servoing, and later learning-based manipulation experiments.

03 · BioMedical Mechatronics Lab · Georgia Tech

MRI-Compatible Needle Intervention Platform

Open interactive CAD ↗ Full project page ↗

Project scope

Designed and prototyped a compact, modular needle-positioning platform for image-guided intervention research. The mechanism has to fit on the patient while preserving access, needle orientation, and room for imaging hardware.

Mechanical work

Developed multi-DOF positioning concepts including actuator linkages, pivot constraints, ball-joint interfaces, worm-drive concepts, and a removable needle-insertion module. I also reverse-engineered older pneumatic actuator concepts before redesigning the surrounding structure.

Main difficulty

Packaging is the central constraint. Patient geometry, MRI bore clearance, receive coils, actuator placement, needle path, and manufacturability all compete for the same small volume. Changes to one motion axis often force changes elsewhere in the platform.

Validation boundary

The current work is a research prototype and mechanical feasibility platform. MR material choices, heating, artifact behavior, and final safety classification still require formal validation before the system could be described as MR Safe or MR Conditional.
