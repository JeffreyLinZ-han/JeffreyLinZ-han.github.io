OpenArm berry harvesting

A simulation of an OpenArm robot using roller grippers to contact, detach, and collect berries.

Interactive twin →

Used. The existing OpenArm platform, the original RRP robot's roller grippers, and RealSense D435 camera assemblies. The model uses MJCF/URDF, elastic belt segments, flexible stems, and a load-and-fatigue attachment model.

Built. I aligned the CAD and joint frames, integrated the grippers on both wrists, and implemented contact, detachment, and recorded simulation playback. MuJoCo runs the demonstration; an optional Newton backend uses converted scene assets. The project also retains the earlier RRP workstation.

Status. This preset collects 2 of 3 ripe berries while all 5 unripe berries remain attached. The eight-fruit cluster carries the physical interactions; the surrounding bush is visual context. Motion is scripted. Camera-based recognition and a picking policy remain future work.
