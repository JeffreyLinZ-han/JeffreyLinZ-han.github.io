01 / System & contribution

Making the parts
work together.

The OpenArm platform and roller gripper come from existing hardware designs. My work is their integration and simulation: consistent geometry, motion, contact, and recorded behavior.

My roleRobot integration
Simulation development
01

Connect the assemblies

The original RRP platform's roller gripper is mounted on both OpenArm wrists. I carry the CAD geometry into MJCF and URDF, align the joint and mesh frames, and retain the upright RealSense D435 housing, mounting bracket, and optical frames.

02

Model the picking contact

Segmented elastic bands run around the rollers and contact the modeled fruit. Flexible stems and a load-and-fatigue attachment model determine when a berry releases. Terminal logs report picked fruit, unripe drops, and peak squeeze estimates.

03

Build a repeatable workflow

The project supports MuJoCo and an optional Newton backend through converted scene assets. The demonstration shown here runs in MuJoCo. Preset motions and recorded simulation states make the contact sequence available from several camera views.

Next research step

Choose the force,
direction, and speed.

The next step is a policy that selects the gripping force and roller direction and speed, then adjusts the action as contact changes.

The current sequence uses prescribed commands. The D435 model and camera views are in place; learned control and camera-based fruit recognition remain future work.

The full bush provides visual context. The eight-fruit target cluster carries the simulated contact, stem, and detachment behavior.

03 / Earlier platform

The original
RRP workstation.

This work began with an existing revolute–revolute–prismatic harvesting robot. I processed more than 100 exported STL components, rebuilt the articulated hierarchy, and corrected mesh origins and coordinate frames for simulation.

That platform is preserved as the project's history. The linked browser model shows the original RRP robot, including its telescoping arm and wrist controls.
