Simulation

Make the geometry tell the truth.

The first challenge was not planning a clever grasp. It was getting a large inherited assembly to agree on something as basic as where its axes actually were.

I restructured the model around a clean joint hierarchy, fixed coordinate-frame and mesh-orientation problems, and turned a large pile of exported geometry into a simulation-ready manipulator. The result is now a base for inverse kinematics, motion planning, fruit perception, visual servoing, and learning-based manipulation.

Touch the model yourself.
