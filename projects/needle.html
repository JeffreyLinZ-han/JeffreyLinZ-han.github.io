MRI needle intervention

A compact needle-positioning mechanism designed for image-guided intervention research.

Interactive twin →

Used. SolidWorks and rapid prototyping to study a modular positioning mechanism and its actuator geometry.

Built. My mechanical work includes actuator linkages, pivot constraints, ball-joint interfaces, worm-drive concepts, and a removable needle-insertion module. I also reverse-engineered earlier pneumatic actuator concepts. The layout accounts for patient access, MRI bore clearance, receive coils, and the needle path.

Status. This is a mechanical research prototype. Clinical use and MR safety classification have not been validated. The interactive model shows the complete assembly and the isolated actuator subsystem.
