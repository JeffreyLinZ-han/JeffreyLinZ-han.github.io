Mechanical design

Spend every millimeter carefully.

The platform is built around compact, modular motion for image-guided needle positioning. The design work meant balancing patient fit, angular range, actuator geometry, MR compatibility, manufacturability, and the need to swap subsystems without rebuilding the entire robot.

I worked through actuator concepts, pivot constraints, ball-joint interfaces, transmission geometry, and a removable needle-insertion module. The project has been a useful lesson in designing mechanisms around a clinical environment instead of designing the mechanism first and finding a use later.
