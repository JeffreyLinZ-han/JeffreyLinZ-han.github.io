MRI-Compatible Needle Intervention

Mechanical architecture for a modular image-guided needle-placement platform, developed around patient fit, constrained workspace, actuator packaging, manufacturability, and MRI-compatible system requirements.

Design contribution

Compact motion under clinical and system constraints.

Open interactive CAD

Mechanism development

  • Iterated modular platform architecture, patient-conforming fixture concepts, and needle insertion and tilt mechanisms.
  • Evaluated lead-screw and gear transmission concepts, actuator linkages, pivot geometry, ball-joint interfaces, and removable insertion modules.
  • Reserved interfaces for sensing, image guidance, and later closed-loop control while keeping subsystems replaceable.
  • Used rapid-prototyped components and CAD reviews to assess packaging, assembly, manufacturability, and integration.

Primary constraints

The mechanism must fit around the patient and imaging workflow, provide useful angular and insertion range, remain serviceable, and avoid design choices incompatible with the MRI environment. These constraints drive the architecture before actuator optimization or control development.

Current boundary: the documented contribution is mechanical design and system architecture. Image guidance and autonomous needle placement remain future integration work.
