MRI-Compatible Needle Intervention

A needle-positioning robot designed around constraints that do not bend: MRI compatibility, compact geometry, precise motion, and hardware that still has to be buildable.

Mechanical design

Motion inside
hard constraints.

I worked on a modular MRI-compatible needle intervention platform for image-guided procedures. The design process started by studying earlier pneumatic actuator concepts, then rebuilding the mechanical pieces around manufacturability, modularity, and integration with MR-safe hardware.

The mechanism brings together multi-DOF needle positioning, actuator linkages, ball-joint interfaces, pivot constraints, transmission concepts, and a needle-insertion module that can be treated as its own subsystem.
