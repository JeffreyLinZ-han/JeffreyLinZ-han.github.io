Custom 6-DOF Robotic Arm

A from-scratch robotic arm project spanning mechanical design, embedded control, encoder feedback, a MuJoCo digital twin, inverse kinematics, and handwriting trajectory execution.

STM32PythonMuJoCoSolidWorksTrajectory PlanningEncoder Feedback
System

From CAD to control.

I designed and assembled the arm around custom 3D-printed joints, timing-belt reductions, bearings, shafts, and modular actuator mounts. The shoulder and elbow use stepper-driven reductions, while the wrist uses servo actuation. An STM32 controller handles low-level motion and serial commands from a Python desktop interface.

The software stack grew into a MuJoCo digital twin and trajectory-planning pipeline. The simulated end effector can reproduce single-stroke handwriting paths, while the hardware side has been extended toward absolute-encoder feedback and closed-loop joint execution.

Engineering challenges

  • Integrated STM32 stepper control with servo and encoder communication across separate serial interfaces.
  • Debugged encoder communication layer-by-layer: UART configuration, raw packet parsing, echo rejection, cable pinout, device IDs, and hardware reliability.
  • Built a digital-twin workflow connecting robot geometry, joint limits, inverse kinematics, trajectory generation, and physical execution.
  • Evaluated torque, reduction ratio, belt tension, backlash, shaft support, and structural stiffness through repeated mechanical iterations.
