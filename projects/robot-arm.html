W-D1 robotic arm

A six-axis desktop arm with STM32 control, absolute joint sensing, and a MuJoCo model.

Interactive twin →

Used. SolidWorks, 3D-printed structures, timing-belt reductions, bearings, stepper and servo actuators, and absolute encoders. STM32 handles low-level commands, with a Python serial interface and MuJoCo for simulation.

Built. I designed the joints and housings, assembled the transmission, and implemented the joint-command interface. Mechanical revisions addressed belt tension, shaft support, cable routing, and encoder zeroing. The MuJoCo model follows the same joint layout as the physical arm.

Status. The physical prototype runs repeatable demonstration motions. The simulated arm can execute handwriting paths. Closed-loop joint execution and a reliable mapping between commanded and measured joint angles are still being developed.
